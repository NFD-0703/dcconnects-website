SOUTH KOREA

South Korea is one of the most digitally connected countries in the world, with internet penetration over 97% and mobile internet usage at 93.6%. Combined with a tech-forward economy and the highest social media usage in the Asia Pacific, it offers a highly attractive environment for digital business expansion. Its data center market is seeing strong growth, driven by rising demand for cloud services, data-heavy applications, and enhanced cybersecurity. The market is projected to more than quadruple in size by 2029, with participation from both local and global players.

JAPAN

Japan’s data center market is growing rapidly, fueled by rising demand for cloud computing, big data analytics, and scalable, dependable IT infrastructure. As the second-largest market in Asia and third globally, Japan benefits from advanced infrastructure, cutting-edge technology (especially in AI), strategic geography, and strong government backing. Hyperscale developments are especially active in key urban hubs like Greater Tokyo and Osaka.

TAIWAN

Taiwan’s data center market is projected to reach USD 2.94 billion by 2030, growing at a CAGR of 8.05%, driven by digitalization, cloud adoption, and 5G expansion. As a global semiconductor leader and tech hub, Taiwan offers a strong foundation for infrastructure growth.

The Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), aligned with GDPR and CBPR, governs data use, while the government pushes sustainability initiatives, targeting net-zero emissions by 2050. With 79% social media penetration and increasing demand for green energy, Taiwan is fostering a robust digital environment. The country has 12 submarine cables, with four more expected by 2027. Major telcos include Chunghwa Telecom, Taiwan Mobile, and Far EasTone. Global cloud providers like AWS, Microsoft, and Google Cloud are actively expanding, with AWS launching the Asia Pacific (Taipei) Region in 2025.
