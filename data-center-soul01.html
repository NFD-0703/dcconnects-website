Location (YongIn, South Korea)

Located in Cheoin-gu, Yongin-si, Gyeonggi-do, South Korea, DCC Soul 1 is strategically positioned near Suwon, Seongnam and Osan, areas with growing demand for data center capacity.

Key Design Features

Building Overview

  • Location: Confidential
  • Buildings: 2
  • Services Type: Cloud + AI
  • Gross Floor Area: 57,971 sqm
  • Typical Floor Height: 7.2m
  • Loading Docks: 2
  • Cargo Lift(5,000kg): 2 for each building
  • Service Lift(5,000kg): 1 for each building
  • Service Lift(2,100kg): 2 for each building
  • Seismic Design: Level IV (IBC Code)

Design Power Capacity

  • Design PUE (Peak): 1.3
  • IT Capacity (Total): 56MW
    - 28MW per building
  • IT Capacity (Data Hall): 4.66MW
    - 6 DHs per building
    - Total 12 DHs

Fire Detection & Suppression System

  • Smoke Detector + Heat Detector, VESDA
  • Siren + Strobe
  • Pre-action valve sprinkler system
  • 2hr fire-rated partitions + 1hr fire-rated doors
  • 24x7 centralized fire alarm monitoring

Power System

  • 2 diverse 154kV KEPCO power
  • 2(N) + 1 utility power supply
  • N + 2 catcher generator with underground fuel tank
  • 24hrs of support at full load
  • Designed to UTI Tier III standard
  • Concurrently maintainable

Connectivity

  • Carrier-neutral
  • 2 main distribution frame(MDF) rooms
  • 4 Meet Me Rooms (2 MMRs in each building)
  • 4 diverse fiber point of entry to site
  • Access to multiple carriers, internet service and cloud service providers

Security

  • Perimeter surveillance with video content analytics
  • 4 graded security area
  • K4 rated car-crash barrier and bollards
  • 24x7 CCTV recording at 1080p resolution
  • 24x7 on-site manned guard
  • Contactless card access and biometric access system
  • 24x7 controlled site access with 2-factor authentication by pre-authorization
