AI-Ready. Efficient. Always Cool.

Scaling AI requires more than compute power—it needs thermal control. Our intelligent cooling systems are built for consistency and performance, ensuring your infrastructure stays efficient and reliable under pressure.

Cooling Tailored to AI Workloads

AI workloads vary—and so do their thermal needs. We offer hybrid cooling options that blend air and liquid technologies, allowing you to customize based on density, environment, and efficiency goals.

Direct-to-Chip Liquid Cooling

Delivers coolant directly to CPU/GPU cold plates, enabling efficient, space-saving heat removal—ideal for high-performance AI while lowering energy use.

Air-Cooled Aisles

Optimized airflow targets high-heat zones, offering reliable cooling for dense deployments while cutting energy costs.

Heat Exchanger

Integrated into rack doors, these systems absorb heat directly from equipment, maintaining optimal temperatures and extending hardware life.
